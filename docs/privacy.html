Last updated: June 16, 2026

League Keeper is a local-first iOS app for tracking Magic: The Gathering budget leagues. Your league data stays on your device.

1. Summary

  • On your device: players, tournaments, pods, standings, achievements, and settings.
  • Sent from App Store (Release) builds only: limited anonymous usage analytics and crash reports via Google Firebase.
  • Not collected: advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, or an in-app account.

2. Data on your device

All league data is stored locally using Apple’s SwiftData. We do not operate a cloud sync service.

3. Third-party services (Release builds)

In App Store Release builds, the app may send allowlisted diagnostic events to Google Firebase:

  • Analytics — product-health events such as app_open, tournament_created, round_recorded. No player names or freeform notes are sent.
  • Crashlytics — crash stack traces and allowlisted non-fatal errors to diagnose failures.

Debug and TestFlight developer builds typically do not enable Firebase collection unless explicitly opted in.
